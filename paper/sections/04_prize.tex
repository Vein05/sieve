\section{The Prize, Measured Honestly}
\label{sec:prize}

Total oracle headroom over the best fixed policy: $65.8 - 52.8 =
\textbf{13.0}$pp. The reader-identity increment (per-(row, reader) oracle over
reader-blind per-row oracle) is \textbf{$+3.4$pp} [95\% CI 2.9--4.0] naively ---
but 4.3\% of cells carry judge-disagreement flags, and dropping them shrinks the
increment to \textbf{$+2.6$pp}, while injecting \emph{random} label flips into
flagged cells inflates it to $+4.5$pp. Hindsight per-reader oracles
preferentially harvest label noise; published headroom estimates built this way
(including our own prior $\sim$30\%-of-headroom figure) are biased upward by
construction. So the prize is 20--26\% of total headroom before asking whether
any of it is reachable.
