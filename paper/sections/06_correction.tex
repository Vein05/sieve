\section{What This Corrects}
\label{sec:correction}

Our prior ``reader-blind oracle captures only $\sim$70\% of headroom'' framing
\citep{panthi2026fixed} is technically true and practically misleading: the
residual is partly judge noise harvested by hindsight oracles
(\S\ref{sec:prize}) and otherwise unreachable by any realizable policy
(\S\ref{sec:results}). Corrected summary: on this action space, reader identity
is worth $\le0.3$pp to any policy we could construct, under both query holdout
and reader-family holdout --- and the ensemble bound shows this is a property of
the signal, not of our policy class: any capability vector, familiarity score,
or routing embedding is a compression of strictly less information than the
donors' realized outcomes that already fail.
